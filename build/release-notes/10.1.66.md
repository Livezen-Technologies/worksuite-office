# WorkSuite Office 10.1.66

## New Features

- **Project Overview report in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **View › Project Overview** for a one-page summary of your plan: how much is complete, when it will finish compared with your baseline, what it will cost compared with the baseline cost, and how many tasks are not started, in progress or done. Below that are the milestones coming up, the tasks running late or past their deadline, and each person's hours and cost. Click a task in the report to select it.
