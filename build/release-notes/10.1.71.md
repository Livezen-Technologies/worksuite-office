# WorkSuite Office 10.1.71

## New Features

- **Split tasks in WorkSuite Project.** When work on a task has to stop for a while and pick up again later, select it and choose **Task › Split task**. Say after how many days of work it pauses and for how long. The Gantt bar shows the two pieces joined by a dotted line, the finish date moves out, and the people on it are free during the pause. **Join split** puts the task back together.

## Known Issues

- Splits are not yet saved in Project XML files; the task keeps its duration there.
