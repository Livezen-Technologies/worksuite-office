# WorkSuite Office 10.1.77

## New Features

- **Formulas in custom fields.** When you add a custom field in WorkSuite Project, you can now give it a formula, and it works itself out for every task. Refer to fields by name in square brackets, for example `[Cost] * 1.15` for cost with tax, `Days([Deadline], [Finish])` for days past the deadline, or `IIf([% Complete] = 100, "Done", "Open")`.
- **Indicators.** Show an icon instead of a number, such as a traffic light: `red if > 5; yellow if > 0; green`. Late or over-budget tasks stand out at a glance.
- **Edit field.** Change a custom field's name, formula or indicators at any time from **Project › Edit field**.
