# WorkSuite Office 10.1.114

## New Features

- **Activate Pro with your key.** Settings › WorkSuite Office Pro now walks you through activation: enter the WSO-… key from your purchase and WorkSuite Office checks it with WorkSuite and unlocks Pro on this computer. It needs the internet once; after that Pro works offline.

## Known Issues

- Activation needs the WorkSuite licence key pinned in this build; until it is, keys are accepted only by builds set up with WS_AI_LICENSE_KEYS.
