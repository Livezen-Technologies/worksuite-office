# WorkSuite Office 10.1.64

## New Features

- **Group by in WorkSuite Project.** Use **View › Group by** to see your tasks under headings: by phase, by person, by status (not started, in progress, done), by priority, or critical versus not critical. Each heading shows how many tasks it has, their dates and how far along they are, and you can collapse it. Grouping by person is a quick way to see everyone's list of work. Groups work together with filters.
