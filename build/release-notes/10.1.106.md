# WorkSuite Office 10.1.106

## New Features

- **P&ID and site plan templates.** **File › From Template** now offers a piping and instrumentation diagram and a site plan drawn to a 1:500 scale.
- **Process and site shapes.** Two new libraries in the shape panel: Process (P&ID) and Site plan.

## Improvements

- Connectors keep their own line width and can be dashed, also when saved as .vsdx.
