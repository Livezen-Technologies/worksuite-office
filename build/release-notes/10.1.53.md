# WorkSuite Office 10.1.53

## New Features

- **WBS codes in WorkSuite Project.** A new **WBS** column numbers every task by its place in the outline (1, 1.1, 1.2, 2…). The codes are also in Excel and CSV exports.
- **Deadlines.** Type a date in the **Deadline** column to say when a task must be finished. A deadline doesn't move the task. If the task finishes later, its deadline turns red, a red marker appears in the Gantt chart, and the status bar counts the tasks that are past their deadline.
- **Inactive tasks.** Select tasks and choose **Task › Inactivate** to keep them in the plan without scheduling them. This is useful for optional work or ideas you might drop. Inactive tasks are greyed out and are not counted in dates, links, workload or progress. Choose **Inactivate** again to bring them back.
- Deadlines and inactive tasks are kept when you save as Microsoft Project XML or open a Project XML file.
