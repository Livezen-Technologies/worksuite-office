# WorkSuite Office 10.1.19

## New Features

- **Recognise an area or an image.** In **Recognise text (OCR)**, choose **An area of the current page** and drag over the part you want, or **An image file** to read a photo or a screenshot.
- **Check before you copy.** Choose **Show the text to check and copy**: the recognised text opens in a window where the words the recognition was unsure of are marked. Correct them there, then **Copy text**.
- **More languages.** **Add languages…** in the Recognise text dialog adds any of 27 more languages, including Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Malayalam, Arabic, Urdu, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, French, German and Spanish. Each language is downloaded once and checked; English, Sinhala and Tamil are built in.

## Known Issues

- Adding languages needs the WorkSuite Local AI service and an internet connection for the download.
- Corrections made while checking the text are in the copied text only; they do not change the PDF.
