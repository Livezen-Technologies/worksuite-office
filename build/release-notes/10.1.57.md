# WorkSuite Office 10.1.57

## New Features

- **Resource levelling in WorkSuite Project.** When someone has too much work at once, choose **Resource › Level All**. WorkSuite Project delays the less important task until that person is free. It never changes anyone's hours. Tasks that don't clash stay exactly where they are.
- **Priority.** A new **Priority** column (0 to 1000, 500 by default) decides which task waits: the lower the number, the sooner it gives way. Tasks that levelling moved show their delay when you point at their priority.
- **Clear Levelling** takes the delays out again.
- Priorities and levelling delays are kept in Microsoft Project XML files.
