# WorkSuite Office 10.1.6

## New Features

- **Smart Typing.** Word suggestions in English and Sinhala as you type, in documents, presentations and spreadsheet cells. With Sinhala off you get English suggestions; with it on, Singlish becomes Sinhala (kanna → කන්න).
- **Smart Mixed mode** decides English or Singlish word by word, so "meeting eka" becomes meeting එක.
- **Spelling and grammar hints** for English (recieve → receive, a/an, repeated words).
- **Suggestion settings** in Sinhala Tools › Suggestions, with a personal dictionary that learns your words.

## Improvements

- Tab accepts a suggestion; Enter and Space keep working normally.
- Optional local AI suggestions appear after a short pause without interrupting typing.

## Bug Fixes

- Suggestions no longer stop when Sinhala input is turned off.
- No suggestions while a spreadsheet formula is being typed.

## Known Issues

- In presentations and spreadsheet cells, Enter with nothing selected only closes the suggestions, next-word suggestions open with Ctrl/Cmd+Space, and an accepted word can take more than one undo step.
- Suggestions are shown in the popup; text is not previewed inline in the document.
