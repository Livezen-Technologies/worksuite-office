# WorkSuite Office 10.1.21

## New Features

- **Sinhala Text Conversion on the right-click menu.** Select text and right-click: **Convert to Sinhala Unicode**, **Convert Unicode to Legacy Font**, **Auto Detect & Convert**, **Select Target Font** (your choice is remembered) and **Open Advanced Converter**.
- **Keyboard shortcuts.** **Ctrl+Shift+U** (Cmd+Shift+U on a Mac) converts the selection to Sinhala Unicode; **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+L** (Cmd+Option+Shift+L) converts it to your legacy font. Ctrl+Shift+L is left as the editors' bulleted list and filter.

## Improvements

- **Convert Sinhala Text** shows the original and the converted text side by side, says which way the selection should be converted, and has **Refresh fonts** for a font you have just installed.

## Bug Fixes

- Converting Unicode to DL Manel and its family (DL-Sarala, Kumudu and others) now writes the rakaransaya (as in ප්‍ර) instead of leaving broken letters.
- When the legacy font is not installed, the preview shows what the converted text will read as, instead of Latin letters that look like a successful conversion.
- Punctuation and English letters that a legacy font shows as Sinhala letters (for example a comma, which FMAbhaya shows as ල) are listed with what they will become before you apply.

## Known Issues

- Legacy fonts have no glyph for some punctuation (such as the comma and full stop in FMAbhaya and DL Manel): it is kept but shows as another letter, and the dialog lists it.
- DL Malithi has no conversion map yet.
- On Linux, Ctrl+Shift+U may be taken by the system's Unicode input (IBus) before it reaches the editor.
