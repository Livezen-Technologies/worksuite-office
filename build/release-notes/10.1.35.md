# WorkSuite Office 10.1.35

## Improvements

- **WorkSuite Document Engine.** Messages in the editors that asked you to contact your "Document Server administrator", for example when you lack rights for an action, a file is too large or fonts did not load, now name the WorkSuite Document Engine, the engine that opens, edits and saves your documents.

## Known Issues

- Languages that translate "Document Server" into their own words keep their translation for now.
- The About screen still credits ONLYOFFICE, whose engine WorkSuite Document Engine is built on.
