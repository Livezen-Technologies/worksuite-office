# WorkSuite Office 10.1.42

## New Features

- **Diagrams inside your documents.** In a document, spreadsheet or presentation, open **Plugins** and choose **WorkSuite Diagram** to draw a flowchart, org chart or network diagram without leaving the file. Click **Insert** and the diagram appears as a picture. Double-click it later to open the diagram again, change it, and click **Insert** to update it. Anyone can open a diagram to look at it; changing it is part of WorkSuite Office Pro.

## Known Issues

- The diagram in a document is a single page; extra pages are kept with it but only the first is shown.
