# WorkSuite Office 10.1.24

## New Features

- **WorkSuite Office everywhere.** The app, its installers and the editors say WorkSuite Office instead of ONLYOFFICE, in every language the app speaks: menus, dialogs, file types, the update service and the editors' window titles.
- **Links that lead to WorkSuite.** Help opens https://worksuitecloud.com/help, Feedback & Support opens https://worksuitecloud.com/support, and the download and website links go to https://download.worksuitecloud.com and https://worksuitecloud.com.

## Improvements

- The About window keeps its attribution: WorkSuite Office is based on ONLYOFFICE © Ascensio System SIA, under the GNU AGPL v3.

## Bug Fixes

- The start page no longer offers ONLYOFFICE's online template gallery or the Box and Dropbox connections that ran on ONLYOFFICE's servers.

## Known Issues

- The ONLYOFFICE logo still appears in some places (the editors' About panel and the embedded viewers' header); a decision on it is pending.
