# WorkSuite Office 10.1.62

## New Features

- **Filters in WorkSuite Project.** Use **View › Filter** to see only the tasks you care about right now: incomplete, completed, critical, late or active tasks, milestones, tasks past their deadline, or everything one person is assigned to. The phases above each task stay in view so you keep your bearings. Choose **Clear filter** to see the whole plan again.
