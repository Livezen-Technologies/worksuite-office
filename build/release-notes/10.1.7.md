# WorkSuite Office 10.1.7

## New Features

- **Sign in with WorkSuite Cloud.** Use your Personal WorkSuite Cloud account in WorkSuite Office. You sign in in your web browser at account.worksuitecloud.com, with two-step verification or a passkey if you use them; WorkSuite Office never sees your password.
- **Your personal cloud in WorkSuite Office.** After you sign in, your WorkSuite Cloud workspace opens by itself and its Drive is on the home screen. Open, edit and save your cloud documents right here; every save is a new version.
- **WorkSuite Cloud in Settings.** Settings › WorkSuite shows your WorkSuite Cloud account, its workspace and how much storage you use, with Open my cloud and Sign out of WorkSuite Cloud.

## Improvements

- If your workspace is still being set up, or your email address is not confirmed yet, WorkSuite Office tells you so.
- Signing out of WorkSuite Cloud also signs its workspace out of WorkSuite Office.

## Known Issues

- Opening your workspace takes a second visit to the browser after signing in to WorkSuite Cloud. It normally finishes by itself; if your workspace asks, choose Continue.
- WorkSuite Cloud is not offered when your organisation has set the server WorkSuite Office signs in to.
- The new WorkSuite Cloud texts are in English in the Sinhala and Tamil versions for now.
