# WorkSuite Office 10.1.76

## New Features

- **Team Planner in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **View › Team Planner** to see who is doing what, and when. Each person has a row with their tasks laid out across the calendar, and tasks no one has been given sit in their own row. Days when someone has too much to do are shaded red. To balance the work, drag a task from one person to another; drag it onto **Unassigned** to take someone off it.
