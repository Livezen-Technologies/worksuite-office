# WorkSuite Office 10.1.74

## New Features

- **Project budget in WorkSuite Project.** Type an amount in **Project › Budget**. The status bar shows what the plan costs against it, and how much over budget you are, and the Project Overview compares the cost with both your budget and your baseline.
- **Cost accrual.** On the **Resource Sheet**, choose when each resource's cost counts as spent: at the **Start** of a task (a deposit, equipment bought up front), **Prorated** as the work gets done, or at the **End** (paid on completion). Earned value's actual cost and planned value follow your choice. It is kept when you exchange Project XML files with Microsoft Project.
