# WorkSuite Office 10.1.36

## New Features

- **WorkSuite Project and WorkSuite Diagram, coming to WorkSuite Office.** Home now shows two more quick actions, **Project** and **Diagram**. Each opens a page that tells you what the app can do today and what is coming next. WorkSuite Project will plan work on a timeline, with tasks, dependencies, milestones and the people who do them. WorkSuite Diagram will draw flowcharts, org charts and network diagrams.

- **Open Visio drawings.** On the Diagram page, **Open a Visio drawing** opens VSDX, VSD and VSDM files to view and print them.

## Improvements

- **A calmer "Session expired" panel.** When your WorkSuite Drive session expires, the account panel now matches the light or dark theme instead of turning orange, and says it once, with a clear **Reconnect** button.

## Known Issues

- You cannot create projects or diagrams yet. Creating and editing them comes in the next stages.
