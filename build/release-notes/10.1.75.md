# WorkSuite Office 10.1.75

## New Features

- **Tracking Gantt in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **View › Tracking Gantt** to see how the plan is going against your baseline. Each task's bar sits over a grey bar showing where it was planned, with how much is done and how many days late or early it will finish. Tasks that will finish later than planned turn amber, so slipping work stands out.
