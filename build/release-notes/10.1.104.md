# WorkSuite Office 10.1.104

## New Features

- **Comments in Visio files.** Saving a diagram as .vsdx now keeps its comments, on their shapes or pages, with author, time and resolved state; replies follow in the comment text. Opening a .vsdx brings its comments in.

## Known Issues

- Visio has no reply threads, so replies come back as part of the first comment's text.
