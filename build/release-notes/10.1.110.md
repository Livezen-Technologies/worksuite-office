# WorkSuite Office 10.1.110

## New Features

- **My shapes.** Keep the shapes you use often: select them and choose **Insert › Add to My shapes**. They appear at the top of the shape panel and travel with the diagram. Click or drag one to use it; right-click to remove it.
- **Visio stencils.** **Insert › Open stencil** brings the shapes of a Visio stencil (.vssx) into My shapes, and **File › Stencil (VSSX)** saves My shapes as a stencil.

## Known Issues

- Connector masters in a stencil are left out, and grouped masters come in as their outline only.
