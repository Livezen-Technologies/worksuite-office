# WorkSuite Office 10.1.84

## New Features

- **Shape data in WorkSuite Diagram.** Give any shape your own information: select it and, under **Shape data** in the panel, add values such as Owner, Cost, Status or Due date. Tick **Show on the shape** to see them under it, or hover the shape. Shape data is kept in Visio files both ways, so the data in your existing Visio drawings now comes across when you open them.
