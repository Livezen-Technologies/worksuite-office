# WorkSuite Office 10.1.37

## Improvements

- **Ready for WorkSuite Project and WorkSuite Diagram.** WorkSuite Office now knows how the two coming apps are licensed: anyone can open, read, print and export projects and diagrams, and WorkSuite Office Pro adds creating and editing them. If your Pro licence ends, your files still open, read-only. There is nothing new to see yet; the apps arrive in a later update.

## Bug Fixes

- **Signature details in signed PDFs.** Now and then a signed PDF showed its signature as not intact and without the issuer of the signer's certificate, although nothing was wrong with it. WorkSuite Office now reads every signature correctly.
