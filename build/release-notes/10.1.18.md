# WorkSuite Office 10.1.18

## New Features

- **Digital signatures.** The PDF Tools tab has a new **Sign** button:
  - **Sign with a certificate file** signs a copy of the PDF with your .p12 or .pfx certificate, optionally with a signature box on the page showing who signed, when and why.
  - **Check signatures** tells you, for each signature in a PDF, who signed it and whether the PDF has changed since.
- **Page size.** Crop the margins of every page, or resize all pages to A4, Letter, A3, A5 or Legal.
- **Version history.** WorkSuite Office keeps a version of each PDF on your computer when you open it with changes since last time, and whenever you choose **Save a version now**. **Version history** opens an earlier version as a copy next to the PDF.

## Known Issues

- Signing uses certificate files (.p12 or .pfx). Certificates in the Windows certificate store, the macOS Keychain or on USB tokens are not supported yet.
- Check signatures does not say whether the signer's certificate is trusted, only whether the PDF has changed since it was signed.
- Sign last: any change after signing, such as stamping or filling a form, breaks the signature.
- These tools need the WorkSuite Local AI service. Versions are kept only while the service is running.
