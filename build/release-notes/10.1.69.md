# WorkSuite Office 10.1.69

## New Features

- **Material and cost resources in WorkSuite Project.** Not every resource is a person. On the **Resource Sheet**, set a resource's type to **Material** (such as cement, by the bag) or **Cost** (such as a permit fee or travel). On a task, put the amount in brackets after the name in the Owner column: **Cement [20]** or **Permit fee [15000]**. Materials cost their quantity times their price per unit; cost resources cost the amount you type. They are counted in the task's cost but never in anyone's hours, so they don't make people look over-allocated. They are kept when you exchange Project XML files with Microsoft Project.
