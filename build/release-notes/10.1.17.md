# WorkSuite Office 10.1.17

## New Features

- **PDF to Excel.** The Convert tab has a new **Excel** button. Tables in the PDF become rows and columns, one sheet per page, and amounts such as 1,250.00 are stored as numbers you can add up.
- **PDF to PowerPoint.** The new **PowerPoint** button makes a presentation with one slide per page.
- **Form data.** The PDF Tools tab has a new **Form data** button:
  - **Fill from data file** fills a form from a JSON, CSV or XFDF file and saves a filled copy.
  - **Save answers** saves the answers of a filled form as JSON, CSV or XFDF.
  - **Flatten form** makes the answers part of the page, so they can no longer be changed.

## Known Issues

- Excel needs the PDF to contain text. For a scanned PDF, use **Recognise text** first.
- In PowerPoint, each slide is a picture of the page; its text cannot be edited.
- Excel, PowerPoint and Form data need the WorkSuite Local AI service, and work on PDFs saved on your computer.
