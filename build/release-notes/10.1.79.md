# WorkSuite Office 10.1.79

## New Features

- **Burndown report in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **View › Burndown** to see how much work is left week by week. The blue line is your plan as it stands, the dashed orange line is your baseline, and a dot marks the work actually left at the status date. A dot above the line means you are behind; below it, ahead. Hover a week for its figures, or open the table view.
