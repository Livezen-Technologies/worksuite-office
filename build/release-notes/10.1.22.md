# WorkSuite Office 10.1.22

## New Features

- **A new Recognise Text (OCR) dialog in the PDF Editor.** The settings are in clear sections between a fixed header and footer, and the dialog follows your light or dark theme.
- **Enhance contrast.** A new scan enhancement that makes faint or grey scans darker on whiter paper before the text is recognised.

## Improvements

- **Languages:** search the installed languages; the languages you chose last time are ticked again.
- **Pages and output as cards:** All pages and Current page show the page count; Selected area and Import image are cards too. Choose Searchable PDF, Word document, PDF/A or Extract text.
- **Progress you can follow:** the percentage, the page being recognised (page x of n), what is happening and the elapsed time. Cancel stays available, and the settings are locked until it ends.
- **A clear ending:** the dialog says whether recognition finished, was cancelled or failed. Use **View results** for extracted text, or **Try again**.

## Bug Fixes

- Recognised Arabic and other right-to-left text now reads right to left in the text view.
- A second recognition can no longer be started while one is running.

## Known Issues

- Import image takes PNG, JPEG, BMP, GIF and WebP files, not TIFF, and only in the desktop app.
- There is no estimate of the time remaining: page times vary too much to give a reliable one.
- Adding languages shows each download as it runs, without a percentage.
