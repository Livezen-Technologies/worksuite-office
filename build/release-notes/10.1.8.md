# WorkSuite Office 10.1.8

## New Features

- **Your personal cloud opens in one step.** After you sign in to WorkSuite Cloud, WorkSuite Office opens your WorkSuite Cloud workspace through WorkSuite Cloud itself: no second visit to the browser.

## Improvements

- If your workspace does not know your account yet, or WorkSuite Cloud cannot open it for you, the workspace's own sign-in opens in your browser as before, and you stay signed in to WorkSuite Cloud.

## Known Issues

- Opening your workspace without the browser needs WorkSuite Cloud's workspace gateway. Until it is available for your workspace, the browser opens once as in 10.1.7.
- The owner of a workspace created before this version signs in to it once in the browser; after that it opens directly.
- WorkSuite Cloud is not offered when your organisation has set the server WorkSuite Office signs in to.
- The new WorkSuite Cloud texts are in English in the Sinhala and Tamil versions for now.
