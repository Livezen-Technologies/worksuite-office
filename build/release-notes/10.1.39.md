# WorkSuite Office 10.1.39

## New Features

- **Automatic scheduling in WorkSuite Project.** On the **Schedule** tab, **Schedule now** lays out your plan from the links between tasks, counting only working days. Choose the Standard calendar or the Sri Lanka calendar, which skips the fixed public holidays (Independence Day, the Sinhala and Tamil New Year, May Day and Christmas). Tasks can be set to start no earlier than a date, to start on a date, or to finish no later than a date, and WorkSuite Project tells you when a date cannot be met. **Critical path** shows in red the tasks that would delay the end of the project.

- **Baselines.** **Save baseline** keeps your plan as it is now. Later, **Show baseline** draws the original plan as thin grey bars under the tasks, and selecting a task tells you how many working days it is late or early.

## Bug Fixes

- **Double-click to edit.** Double-clicking a cell in WorkSuite Project or a shape in WorkSuite Diagram now starts editing it. Before, nothing happened, and what you typed next went into the task name.

## Known Issues

- The Sri Lanka calendar includes only the public holidays on fixed dates. Poya days and other holidays that move every year are not included yet.
