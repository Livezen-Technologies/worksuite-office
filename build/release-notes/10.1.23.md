# WorkSuite Office 10.1.23

## New Features

- **WorkSuite Office Terms of Use in the installer.** The Windows installer's licence page shows WorkSuite's own Terms of Use, followed by the complete GNU Affero General Public License, version 3, that covers the source code.
- **The terms stay with the app.** They are installed as TERMS.txt next to LICENSE.txt and 3DPARTYLICENSE.txt, and the Start menu has a **WorkSuite Office Terms of Use** link to https://worksuitecloud.com/terms.

## Improvements

- The installer is WorkSuite-branded throughout: its copyright now reads © Livezen Technologies LLC, based on ONLYOFFICE © Ascensio System SIA and contributors.

## Bug Fixes

- The installer falls back to English, not Russian, when Windows uses a language it has no translation for.

## Known Issues

- The Terms of Use are in English only.
