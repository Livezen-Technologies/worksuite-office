# WorkSuite Office 10.1.59

## New Features

- **Task Board in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **View › Task Board** to see your tasks as cards in three columns: **Not started**, **In progress** and **Done**. Each card shows the task's phase, dates and people, and late tasks are marked. Drag a card to another column to update its progress.
- **Calendar view.** Choose **View › Calendar** to see a month at a time, with the tasks that run on each working day. Use the arrows to move between months, or **Today** to come back.
- Click a card or a task in either view to select it in the task list.
