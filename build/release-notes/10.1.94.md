# WorkSuite Office 10.1.94

## New Features

- **Scale drawings in WorkSuite Diagram.** Choose **Design › Drawing Scale** to draw floor and site plans to scale, such as 1 cm = 1 m (1:100) or 1/4 in = 1 ft. Select a room to see and type its real width and height.
- **Dimension lines.** The **Floor plan** library has a Dimension line that shows its measured length, such as "6 m" or "12 ft 6 in", and keeps it right as you resize it.
- **Print across several sheets.** **File › Print** can now print a big page at actual size across as many sheets of A4, A3, Letter or Legal as it needs, each labelled with its row and column for putting together. Or fit the whole page on one sheet as before.
