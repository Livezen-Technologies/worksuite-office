# WorkSuite Office 10.1.58

## New Features

- **Recurring tasks in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **Task › Recurring** for something that happens regularly, like a weekly status meeting or a monthly report. Say how often it repeats (daily, weekly on the days you pick, or monthly) and when it ends (after a number of times or on a date). WorkSuite Project adds a summary with one task for each time, marked ↻. Point at the ↻ to see the pattern. A time that falls on a weekend or holiday moves to the next working day.
