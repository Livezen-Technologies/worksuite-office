# WorkSuite Office 10.1.63

## New Features

- **Timeline in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **View › Timeline** for a one-glance picture of your plan, ready to share in a status meeting: each phase is a bar across the months, with a thin line showing how much is done, and each milestone is a diamond labelled with its date. A dashed red line marks the status date. Click a bar or a milestone to select it.
