# WorkSuite Office 10.1.82

## New Features

- **Print to scale in WorkSuite Project.** **Print / PDF** now opens a Print dialog. Choose landscape or portrait paper, then either fit the Gantt chart to one page wide (or two, three…), or print it at a scale such as 100% and let it run across as many pages as it needs, to tape together on the wall. You can also print just a date range, such as the next four weeks.
