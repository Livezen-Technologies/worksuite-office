# WorkSuite Office 10.1.29

## New Features

- **Sinhala fonts on Mac.** WorkSuite Office for Mac now includes Noto Sans Sinhala, Noto Serif Sinhala and Malithi Web in the editors. When you open the app, it also adds them to your fonts, so Pages, Word, browsers and other apps can use them too. Fonts you already have are left as they are.

## Known Issues

- On macOS the fonts are added to your own account (~/Library/Fonts), not for every user on the Mac.
- An AI model exported before 10.1.28 does not include its engine. Export it again from a computer running 10.1.28 or later.
- MCP connectors are not available on macOS yet, and OAuth sign-in for MCP servers is coming later (API keys work now).
