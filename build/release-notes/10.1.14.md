# WorkSuite Office 10.1.14

## New Features

- **Protect PDF.** The PDF Tools tab of the PDF editor has a new **Protect** button. Save a copy of the PDF that needs a password to open, or that restricts printing, copying and changes until a permissions password is given, or both. The copy is encrypted with AES-256, saved next to the PDF and opened in a new tab. The PDF itself is not changed.

## Improvements

- The WorkSuite Local AI service can now merge, split, rotate, protect and unlock PDFs and make PDFs from images, on files on your computer. Nothing extra is downloaded.

## Known Issues

- Protect needs the WorkSuite Local AI service. If it is not installed, or your organization has turned it off, Protect shows a message instead.
- Protect asks you to save the PDF first. PDFs opened from a cloud drive must be saved to your computer before they can be protected.
- A forgotten password cannot be recovered.
