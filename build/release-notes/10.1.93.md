# WorkSuite Office 10.1.93

## New Features

- **Check Diagram in WorkSuite Diagram.** Choose **Review › Check Diagram** to check a flowchart or a BPMN process against the usual rules: a start and an end, decisions with labelled ways out, no steps that lead nowhere, no shapes or lines left on their own. Issues are listed beside the page and marked on it; click one to go to its shape. The list updates as you fix things, and an issue you mean to keep can be ignored.
