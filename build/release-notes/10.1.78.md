# WorkSuite Office 10.1.78

## New Features

- **Interim plans in WorkSuite Project.** Between baselines, choose **Project › Interim Plan** to keep a quick snapshot of every task's start and finish, for example at each weekly review. You can keep up to ten. Pick one in **Compare with** to see in the Tracking Gantt how the dates have moved since then.

## Known Issues

- Interim plans are not yet saved in Project XML files.
