# WorkSuite Office 10.1.27

## Improvements

- Works offline without delays. With no internet, including on a Wi-Fi or office network that is not connected to the internet, the Home page shows your saved WorkSuite lists and the offline banners right away instead of waiting for timeouts. Documents on your computer open and save as usual, and Drive documents you edit offline are uploaded when you are back online.

## Bug Fixes

- WorkSuite requests time out after 10 seconds instead of 30, and Drive downloads and uploads stop after 30 seconds with no data, so nothing hangs when the connection drops.

## Known Issues

- AI models, the Sinhala online suggestions, the Update Center and the Help pages still need an internet connection.
- MCP connectors are not available on macOS yet, and OAuth sign-in for MCP servers is coming later (API keys work now).
- "Home" and "All tabs" are in English in every language until they are translated.
