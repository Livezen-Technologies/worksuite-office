# WorkSuite Office 10.1.90

## New Features

- **Auto Align & Space in WorkSuite Diagram.** One click on **Home** or **Arrange** straightens the lines between connected shapes and makes the gaps between them even, for the shapes you select or for the whole page.
- **More ways to align.** Align right, middle and bottom, and Distribute down, join the existing align and distribute buttons on **Arrange**.

## Improvements

- **Swimlane diagrams lay out properly.** **Top to Bottom** and **Left to Right** keep every shape in its lane: the flow runs along the lanes, and the lanes grow to fit it.

## Bug Fixes

- Laying out a page no longer moves lanes, containers and callouts as if they were ordinary shapes. Containers are fitted round their shapes again, and callouts stay beside their shape.

## Known Issues

- Re-layout does not keep the shapes of different containers apart: if the layout mixes them, their containers can overlap. Undo puts the page back.
