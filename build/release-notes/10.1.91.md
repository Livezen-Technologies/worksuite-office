# WorkSuite Office 10.1.91

## New Features

- **Themes in WorkSuite Diagram.** Choose **Design › Theme** to colour a page, or the whole diagram, with one of six themes: Office, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Berry or Slate. Each comes in three variants: light, bold and outline. Decisions, starts and ends, and data shapes each get their own colour, so a flowchart reads at a glance.
- **Backgrounds and borders.** Choose **Design › Background & Border** to give a page a soft background colour and a border with its title: a frame with a title box in the corner, or a band across the top.

## Improvements

- Shapes you have coloured yourself keep their colours when you change the theme. Swimlanes, containers, callouts and floor plans keep theirs too.

## Known Issues

- Backgrounds and borders are not saved into Visio files yet; the theme's shape colours are.
