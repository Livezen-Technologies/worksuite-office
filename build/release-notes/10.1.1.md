## New Features
- WorkSuite Drive in the app: rename, move, copy, move to bin with Undo, version history with Restore, and Share, without opening the browser.
- Switch between your accounts and companies in the WorkSuite Drive header.
- New from template: your company's templates, by category.
- WorkSuite links open with the right account, and mail attachments open as a read-only copy you can save to Drive.
- Settings › WorkSuite: accounts, the default server, where Drive documents open, and updates (Stable or Beta).
- Sinhala input in Sinhala Tools: type Singlish and pick the Sinhala word from suggestions beside the cursor, like Google Input Tools. Turn it on with Ctrl+Shift+Y.
- PDF Editor: recognise text (OCR) in English, Sinhala and Tamil on scanned PDFs, on this computer, and save as PDF/A.
- Windows and Linux update themselves from the WorkSuite Update Center; Intel Macs have their own build.
- Organisations can manage WorkSuite Office settings, which then show as locked.

## Improvements
- WorkSuite says when a server is too old for it.
- Other cloud services are hidden unless your organisation allows them.
- WorkSuite AI is offered as the editors' AI provider when your server has it.
- A cleaner AI agent panel that you can hide.
- WorkSuite's words in Sinhala and Tamil.

## Bug Fixes
- The macOS About window shows the WorkSuite Office icon.
- On Linux, your sign-in is kept in the system keyring instead of a file.

## Known Issues
- PDF to Excel and PowerPoint is not available yet.
- Offline editing of Drive documents is not available yet.
- Sinhala and Tamil words are machine translations awaiting review.
- Until code signing keys are set up, macOS asks before opening the app the first time.
