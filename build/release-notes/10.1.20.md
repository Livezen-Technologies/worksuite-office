# WorkSuite Office 10.1.20

## New Features

- **Sign with the certificates on your computer.** In **PDF Tools › Sign**, choose **A certificate on this computer** and pick from the certificates Windows or macOS keeps for you, including those on a smart card or USB token (for example a national e-signature token). The card or token asks for its PIN in its own window, and its private key never leaves it. Certificate files (.p12, .pfx) still work as before.

## Known Issues

- On Linux, signing still uses certificate files only.
- Only certificates that are allowed to sign documents, and that have their private key on the computer or on a connected card or token, are listed.
