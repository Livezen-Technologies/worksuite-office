## New Features
- Sinhala Tools: a new tab in the editors converts text in the legacy FM Abhaya, DL Manel and Kaputa fonts to Unicode Sinhala and back. Convert a selection, or the whole document in one step that Undo takes back, after a preview of what changes.
- Detect highlights text in legacy fonts, Fonts sets Noto Sans or Noto Serif Sinhala on Sinhala text, and Validate lists broken conjuncts, stray joiners and vowel signs in the wrong order.
- Noto Sans Sinhala and Noto Serif Sinhala now come with WorkSuite Office. Sinhala Tools is in English, Sinhala and Tamil.

## Improvements
- Characters a legacy font map does not know are listed with where they are, and left as they were: nothing is dropped.

## Known Issues
- The legacy font maps cover letters, vowel signs, yansaya and rakaransaya; repaya, touching letters and other special glyphs are listed as unmapped. The maps are checked against WorkSuite's own sample texts only, so check converted text before you share it.
- Convert document, Detect, Fonts and Validate work in documents; in spreadsheets and presentations, convert a selection.
- Windows and Linux are on the 10 editors from this version. The Linux packages need Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer, and there is no .rpm package yet.
- After updating from 9.3 on Windows or Linux, sign in to WorkSuite and to your other clouds again.
- The macOS build is not code signed yet, so macOS asks before opening it the first time.
