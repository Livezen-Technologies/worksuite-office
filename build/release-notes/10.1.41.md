# WorkSuite Office 10.1.41

## New Features

- **Edit Visio drawings in WorkSuite Diagram.** Choose **Open** and pick a Visio drawing (.vsdx) to edit it in WorkSuite Diagram: its pages, shapes, text, colours and the connectors between shapes come across, and connectors stay attached when you move shapes. **Export VSDX** on the File tab saves your diagram as a Visio drawing that colleagues can open in Visio and other diagram tools.

## Known Issues

- Pictures, shape data, hyperlinks and background pages in a Visio drawing are not brought across yet; WorkSuite Diagram tells you when a file has them.
- Groups in a Visio drawing open as separate shapes, and curved outlines made of splines are drawn with straight segments.
- Older Visio files (.vsd) still open in the viewer only.
