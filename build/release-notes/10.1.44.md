# WorkSuite Office 10.1.44

## New Features

- **See who is busy in WorkSuite Project.** Open **View** and tick **Workload** to see how many days of work each person has every week. Type people's names in the **Owner** column; separate several names with commas.
- **Over-allocation warnings.** When someone has two tasks on the same day, the clashing tasks get a red dashed outline in the Gantt chart, their weeks turn red in the workload view, and the status bar names who is over-allocated.

## Known Issues

- Everyone on a task counts as working on it full time; part-time assignments come later.
