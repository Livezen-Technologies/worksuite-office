# WorkSuite Office 10.1.49

## New Features

- **Start a plan from an outline in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **File › From outline…** and type or paste your tasks, one per line. Indent a line to put it under the one above. At the end of a line you can add a duration (**5d**, **2w**), the word **milestone**, and people (**@Nimal**, **@Priya 50%**). WorkSuite Project links the tasks in order and schedules them on the working calendar. Click **Try an example** to see how it works.
- **Turn a table into a diagram in WorkSuite Diagram.** Choose **File › From table…** and paste rows from a spreadsheet:
  - **Name, Role, Reports to** makes an org chart.
  - **From, To, Label** makes a flowchart. Steps ending in "?" become decisions.

  The diagram goes on a new page, already laid out, and one **Undo** removes it.
