# WorkSuite Office 10.1.45

## New Features

- **Track progress against a status date in WorkSuite Project.** On the **Schedule** tab, pick a **Status date**: the day your team last reported progress. A dashed line marks it on the Gantt chart. Tasks that are less far along than they should be get an amber outline, and the status bar shows how many tasks are behind and how many are late.
- **Actual dates.** When you set a task's progress, WorkSuite Project notes when work started; at 100% it notes when it finished. Actual dates and the status date travel with Project XML files, to and from Microsoft Project.

## Known Issues

- Actual dates follow the planned dates; typing a different actual date comes later.
