# WorkSuite Office 10.1.65

## New Features

- **Custom fields in WorkSuite Project.** Choose **Project › Custom Field** to add a column of your own to the task list, such as Supplier, Budget code, Approved or Delivery date. Each field is text, a number, a date or yes/no, and WorkSuite checks what you type. Your fields are saved with the plan and included when you export to CSV or Excel. **Remove field** takes a column away, and Undo brings it back.

## Known Issues

- Custom fields are not yet included in Project XML files.
