# WorkSuite Office 10.1.50

## New Features

- **Draft with AI in WorkSuite Project and WorkSuite Diagram.** Choose **File › Draft with AI…** and describe what you need. For example, "plan a 3-month office move for 40 people", or "how a refund is approved". The local AI writes a first draft. It opens for you to check and change, then becomes your plan or diagram. Everything runs on your computer and nothing is sent anywhere. You need the WorkSuite Local AI assistant model installed and WorkSuite Office Pro.

## Bug Fixes

- In WorkSuite Diagram's auto-layout, a line that skips a step (such as a "No" answer that jumps ahead) now goes round the side instead of cutting through the shapes in between.
