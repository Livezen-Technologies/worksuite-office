# WorkSuite Office 10.1.83

## New Features

- **Swimlanes in WorkSuite Diagram.** Choose **Insert › Swimlanes**, name your teams or roles (for example Customer, Sales, Operations, Finance), and get a cross-functional flowchart with one lane each, across or down the page. Put your steps in the lanes; when you drag a lane, its steps move with it. **Add lane** adds another one at the end. Swimlanes are kept when you export to Visio.
