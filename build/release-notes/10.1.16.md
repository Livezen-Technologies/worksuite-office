# WorkSuite Office 10.1.16

## New Features

- **Cleaner scans before text recognition.** The **Recognise text (OCR)** dialog in the Convert tab has a new **Clean up scans** section:
  - **Straighten tilted pages**, for pages scanned a few degrees off.
  - **Turn sideways and upside-down pages upright**, found by a quick trial recognition of each page.
  - **Make pages black and white**, which removes grey backgrounds and specks and makes the file smaller.

## Improvements

- Recognised pages look sharper: colour pages keep more detail, and black and white pages are stored without JPEG blur.

## Known Issues

- Turning pages upright adds a few seconds per page.
- Recognising a selected area, a review list of uncertain words, and extra languages beyond English, Sinhala and Tamil are planned for a later version.
