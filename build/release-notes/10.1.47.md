# WorkSuite Office 10.1.47

## New Features

- **Auto-layout in WorkSuite Diagram.** On the **Arrange** tab, **Layout ↓** tidies an org chart or tree from top to bottom, with each manager centred over their team. **Layout →** tidies a flowchart from left to right, step by step, arranged so lines cross as little as possible. Select some shapes to tidy only those, or nothing to tidy the whole page. If the result needs more room, the page grows. Changed your mind? One **Undo** puts everything back.

## Bug Fixes

- An elbow line that leaves and joins two shapes on the same side now goes round them instead of cutting through them.
