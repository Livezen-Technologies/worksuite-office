# WorkSuite Office 10.1.34

## New Features

- **Remove hidden information from a PDF.** On the PDF Tools tab, **Remove hidden info** saves a copy without the information you cannot see on the page but someone could still find in the file: the author and other document details, scripts, attached files, comments and highlights, page thumbnails and data left by other programs. Links and form fields stay, and older saved versions hidden inside the file are dropped too. When it is done, WorkSuite Office tells you what it removed. Your original PDF is not changed.

## Known Issues

- Layers in a PDF are kept as they are, including hidden ones. The copy tells you when a PDF has layers.
- A few PDFs have parts WorkSuite Office cannot read. For those, no copy is saved, so you never get a damaged file.
