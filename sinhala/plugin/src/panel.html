Sinhala input

Type Singlish in the document (amma, shrii lankaava) and pick a word: Space or Enter writes the highlighted one, 1-9 that one, the arrow keys move, Esc keeps the Latin. Ctrl+Shift+Y turns it on and off.

Your picks are kept on this computer only and never sent anywhere.

Maps are checked against WorkSuite's own sample texts only. Check converted text before you share it.
